During the internship, I participated in 3 events, each offering memorable experiences with new, insightful knowledge about Cloud Computing and career development in the AI era.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** Swinburne Cloud Mastery – Career Talk Panel

&emsp;**Time:** 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, July 4th, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** Floor 26, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Description:** A career talk panel featuring 4 speakers sharing real-world insights about navigating the tech job market in the AI era, the power of internal referrals, the importance of technical foundations and communication, and a proactive mindset in career development.

&emsp;**Value Gained:** Gained clarity on the AI-driven "inverted hiring pyramid" and the Jensen Paradox. Recognized that visibility, referrals, communication, and genuine reflection (Experience ≠ Seniority) are just as important as technical skills in building a successful tech career.

### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** AWS Study Group Meetup – July 2026

&emsp;**Time:** 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, July 11th, 2026

&emsp;**Location:** Floor 26, Bitexco Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Description:** A multi-topic meetup covering: Cloud Architect competition finals, experience sharing on preparing for the AWS Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) certification, risk management strategies on AWS, and user experience monitoring techniques using CloudWatch RUM and X-Ray.

&emsp;**Value Gained:** Learned an effective 4–6 week AWS CCP study roadmap, understood how to build proactive alerting and cost control systems, and grasped the distinction between monitoring, logging, and tracing — the 3 pillars of modern observability.
