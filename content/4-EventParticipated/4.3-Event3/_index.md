### Key Takeaways (System Architecture Perspective)

From my role as a system architecture designer, this event provided invaluable lessons:
- **Cost & Performance Architecture Optimization (Min/Mid/Max):** The takeaway from Signal Scout regarding detailed architectural modeling and AWS budgeting based on three load levels deepened my understanding of designing more flexible and practical Cloud systems.
- **Solution Architect Workflow Automation:** Plan V's solution for AI-native automatic generation of Draw.io/AWS diagrams and cost estimation opens a new horizon: how AI tools can assist our architecture design workflow, reducing repetitive manual tasks.
- **Practicality and Execution (MVP):** Team 3KA showed that a small, well-implemented product (like combining YOLO, ByteTrack, and AWS Agent with low latency) holds far more value than a large but incomplete idea.

---

### Practical Application

- **Apply the Min/Mid/Max cost estimation model** to my future architecture designs to clearly demonstrate financial feasibility to clients or development teams.
- **Research IaC (Infrastructure as Code) tools and automated architecture diagramming** similar to Plan V's approach to optimize my personal workflow.
- **Adopt the "Completed MVP" mindset** when planning and staging architectural phases, ensuring the core business flow runs smoothly before adding advanced features.
