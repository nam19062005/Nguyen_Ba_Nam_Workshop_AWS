### 🎯 Event Purpose

The **AWS Study Group Meetup** of November 2026 was a special end-of-program gathering focused on:

- Sharing real-world experience from teams that participated in the **hackathon** within the FCAJ program.
- Reviewing the Cloud learning journey throughout the Bootcamp, reflecting on what was achieved.
- Creating networking and exchange opportunities among AWS Study Group community members in Ho Chi Minh City.

---

### 📝 Event Content

#### Hackathon Experience Sharing
- Hackathon teams shared their complete journey: from **ideation, technology stack selection, task division, late-night debugging** to product completion.
- Each team presented **hard-learned lessons**: timeboxing under tight schedules, handling team conflicts, pivoting quickly when initial solutions weren't feasible.
- Highlighted AWS solutions used: **Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, S3, Bedrock, Rekognition** — how to integrate quickly to achieve an MVP in 24–48 hours.

#### Effective Hackathon Strategies
- **Choose the right problem**: Prioritize problems within the team's strength zone — don't try to impress judges by choosing a difficult problem without domain knowledge.
- **MVP first, polish later**: Build the core working parts first, additional features later — avoid the perfectionism trap that causes missed submission deadlines.
- **Demo is everything**: Judges evaluate through a 5-minute demo, prepare a smooth demo script, avoid live coding under pressure.
- **Use AWS to "cheat" legitimately**: AWS managed services dramatically reduce development time compared to building from scratch.

#### Reflecting on the FCAJ Bootcamp Journey
- Mentors and speakers summarized the program: strengths, areas for improvement, and planned changes for the next Bootcamp season.
- Shared upcoming opportunities: **AWS Certification paths, internship, full-time job referrals** from the AWS Study Group ecosystem.

---

### 💡 Key Takeaways

#### Practical Skills
- **Speed Coding Under Pressure**: Witnessed how skilled teams think and execute quickly under time pressure — a skill impossible to learn from books alone.
- **Fast Serverless Architecture**: Learned how to build Cloud-native applications quickly using AWS managed services.
- **Product Mindset**: Understood the difference between "making it work" and "making what users actually need" — business value matters more than technology.

#### Inspiration & Direction
- **Motivation to join the next hackathon**: After hearing the sharing, committed to registering for hackathons in the next Bootcamp season for direct experience.
- **Concrete action plan**: Planned to complete the **AWS Solutions Architect Associate** certification in the next 3 months to prepare for job opportunities.
- **Community is an asset**: Recognized that the AWS Study Group network is as valuable as technical knowledge — will continue to actively participate.
