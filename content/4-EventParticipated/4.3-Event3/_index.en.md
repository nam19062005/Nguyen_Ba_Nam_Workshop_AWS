### Key Highlights

#### Talk 1 – Team 3KA: S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D
- **24-Hour Journey:** Experienced 4 stages: registration, pressure build-up, demo, and lesson reflection.
- **Overview & Features:** Used YOLO, ByteTrack, and AWS Agent for live camera analysis, crowd density measurement, overload prediction, and proactive alerting.
- **Challenges & Memories:** Faced fear of lacking AI/AWS skills, code bugs, and sleep deprivation, but enjoyed fun moments like eating KFC, drinking Redbull, and fixing bugs until 3 AM.

#### Talk 2 – Team Signal Scout: SignalScout
- **Objective:** Collect, correlate scattered signals, and validate data to early detect signs of restructuring or strategic shifts in competitors for risk management support.
- **Architecture & Cost:** Detailed analysis of architecture models and AWS budget estimation (Bedrock, AgentCore, WAF...) based on 3 load levels (Min/Mid/Max) for cost optimization.

#### Talk 3 – Team Plan V: Solution Architect Professional AI Native App
- **Problem:** Traditional SA workflow is time-consuming: reading requirements manually, drafting architectures from scratch, and estimating costs based on personal experience.
- **AI Native Solution:** Automatically analyzes natural language requirements, drafts hybrid-cloud architectures, auto-generates Draw.io/AWS diagrams, and estimates costs for ap-southeast-1 region.

#### Talk 4 – Team OneTeam: OneTeam Community Day Presentation
- **Objective:** Present the team's solutions and contributions within the framework of AWS Community Day and Agentic AI Build Week.
- **Key Activities:** Presented research findings, applied technology to solve community problems, and fostered technology knowledge sharing.

### Key Takeaways

#### From Team 3KA
- **Computer Vision Integration:** How to combine real-time computer vision, object tracking, cloud inference, and Agentic AI.
- **Performance Optimization:** Camera optimization and latency reduction skills under time pressure.
- **Hackathon Preparation:** Preparation for next time: clear goals, ready toolsets, defined roles, and demo rehearsals.

#### From Team Signal Scout
- **Distributed System Management:** Managing multi-service deployment, service discovery, and network handling.
- **CI/CD Pipeline:** Setting up CI/CD pipelines for continuous releases in distributed systems.
- **Cost Optimization:** Detailed cost optimization methodology for each cloud service.

#### From Team Plan V
- **Agentic AI Application:** Utilizing Agentic AI to fully automate a highly specialized and complex professional workflow for Solution Architects.
- **Conversational Interactions:** Shifting from manual infrastructure code (IaC) and diagram creation to automated conversational interactions.

#### From Team OneTeam
- **Team Collaboration:** Organization, teamwork, and optimizing technology solutions to serve the community in a short timeframe.
- **Presentation Skills:** Experience in transferring and presenting ideas before a panel of cloud experts.

### Practical Application

- **Apply the Min/Mid/Max cost estimation model** to my future architecture designs to clearly demonstrate financial feasibility to clients or development teams.
