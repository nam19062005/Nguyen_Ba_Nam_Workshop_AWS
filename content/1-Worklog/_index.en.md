During the 8-week First Cloud AI Journey Bootcamp at AWS Vietnam, the weekly learning roadmap and progress milestones are summarized below:

The AWS self-study roadmap is designed from **Week 1 to Week 8** (from June 8, 2026, to July 31, 2026).
